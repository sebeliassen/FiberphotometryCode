# Master 72-Video Behavioral Sequence Screening Suite

Comprehensive visual screening suite comparing all **8 sequence discovery methods** across **3 candidate families** under **3 distinct exemplar selection paradigms** (72 videos total).

### The 3 Exemplar Selection Paradigms:
1. **No Exemplar (Uniform Spread)** (`screening_24_batch/`): Unbiased sampling spread evenly across the dataset to observe natural inter-session variance.
2. **Whole-Chain Density-Ratio Exemplar** (`screening_24_exemplars/`): Keypoint-MoSeq native contrastive density-ratio algorithm in 4D continuous SLDS latent space ($55\%\text{--}89\%$ variance drop).
3. **Forward Transition-Bucketed Exemplar** (`screening_24_transition_exemplars/`): Evaluates each constituent syllable locally within its forward transition bucket $\mathcal{B}(S_k \to S_{k+1})$, eliminating post-hoc whole-chain circularity.

### Viewing Preset Enforced Across All 72 Videos:
- **40 FPS continuous real-speed** (`sync_mode="nothing"`)
- **Follow-centroid crop** (`centroid_mode="follow_centroid"`)
- **Colored centroid dot directly on mouse** (active syllable color, no badge dot, no yellow transition flash)
- **Solid black canvas padding** (`cv2.BORDER_CONSTANT, (0,0,0)`)
- **Trajectory inset removed** (`show_trajectory_inset=False`)

## 3-Question Review Rubric for Video Inspection

When comparing the 3 video modes for each candidate family, evaluate:

1. **Is the Behavioral Progression Understandable?** (`Clear / Possible / No / Corrupt`)
   - Does the No-Exemplar video reveal too much variance, while the Whole-Chain or Transition-Exemplar video clarifies the progression?
2. **What Appears Invariant Across Modes?**
   - Note consistent motor primitives (e.g. stride cadence, rearing re-orientation, yaw turn angles).
3. **What Distinguishes Whole-Chain vs. Transition-Bucketed?**
   - Does the transition-bucketed mode maintain natural variation in departure direction while keeping each stage crisp?
